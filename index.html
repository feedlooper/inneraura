Meet your facilitator

Hi, I'm Gurleen —
and I'm so glad you're here.

Breathwork found me, and it changed everything. I knew, deep down, that helping others through the breath is my life's passion, and I've devoted myself to it completely.

My approach is gentle, intuitive, and deeply personal. I don't believe in one-size-fits-all healing. I listen, really listen, to what you need from each session, and I hold a warm, judgment-free space for whatever arises. Whether you arrive carrying stress, grief, anxiety, or simply exhaustion, you'll be met with kindness, patience, and care.

My hope is simple: that the calm and healing you find here follows you out the door, into your life, and to everyone you love.

Questions

Everything you might be wondering.

Still curious? Reach out on Instagram. Gurleen loves hearing from you.

I've never done breathwork. Is it for me?+

Absolutely. Most of Gurleen's clients are brand new. Sessions are gentle, guided the whole way through, and always move at your pace. There is nothing to get "right" and no experience needed.

What should I bring or wear?+

Wear comfortable, cozy clothing you can breathe and lie down in. Bring water, and if you like, a journal for afterwards. Everything else is taken care of: mats, blankets, bolsters, and eye masks.

What might I feel during a session?+

Everyone's experience is different: deep relaxation, emotional release, tingling sensations, clarity, or simply the best rest you've had in months. Whatever arises is welcome and normal, and Gurleen stays right beside you through all of it.

Will sessions be covered by insurance?+

Soon! Gurleen is completing her Natural Health Practitioner certification with Hara Breath, after which sessions will be eligible for coverage under many extended health plans. Follow @gugzzr for the announcement.

Where are sessions held?+

Sessions take place in Vancouver, BC. The exact studio location and any preparation details are shared in your booking confirmation.
